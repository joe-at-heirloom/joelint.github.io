How to convert a PDF to JPG on a Mac

To post a page of a PDF, or attach it somewhere that only takes images, you need it as a JPG. Preview can export one page at a time. The Converter turns every page into its own JPG in one go, on your Mac.

With Preview

  1. Open the PDF in Preview and select the page you want in the sidebar.
  2. Choose File › Export.
  3. Set Format to JPEG, choose a resolution, and click Save.

That exports one page. For a twenty-page PDF you do it twenty times.

With The Converter

The Converter is a free, open-source Mac app that converts images, video, audio and documents entirely on your Mac.

  1. Drop the PDF onto The Converter's window.
  2. Pick JPEG.
  3. Click Convert. You get one JPG per page, saved next to the PDF.

Or skip the window: select the files in Finder, Control-click, and choose Services › Convert to JPEG. The new file lands next to the original.

What you get

  • One JPG for every page, numbered in order: report-1.jpg, report-2.jpg, and report-01.jpg onwards once there are ten pages or more, so they sort properly.
  • Rendered at 200 DPI, so a US Letter page comes out 1700 × 2200 pixels.
  • Anything transparent on the page comes out white, as it looks on screen.
  • JPEG quality 90.

The Converter is free and open source. It needs macOS 14 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

Questions

Should I pick JPG or PNG?

For pages that are mostly text or line drawings, PNG keeps the edges of the letters sharp. For pages that are mostly photos, JPG files are much smaller. The Converter does both.

Can I convert just one page?

The Converter does every page. For a single page, Preview's Export (above) is quicker.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your Mac, with no page or file-size limit.
