The Converter

A free, native Mac app that converts images, video, audio, and documents. Drop files in, pick a format, done - and every file stays on your machine.

The difference

Everything happens on your Mac

Most online converters upload your files to someone else's server, cap your file size, meter your daily conversions, or ask you to make an account. The Converter does none of that. It runs the conversion entirely on your machine - so your files never leave it.

No uploads
Files are read and written locally. Nothing is sent anywhere.
No accounts
No sign-up, no email, no login. Open it and go.
No limits
No daily quotas and no file-size caps. Convert as much as you like.
Zero network calls
Fully offline. The app works with your Wi-Fi turned off.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license, forever. If it saves you some hassle and you'd like to chip in, donations are welcome — but they're never required and nothing is locked behind them.

Does anything leave my Mac?

No. Every conversion runs entirely on your machine — no uploads, no accounts, no file-size limits, no analytics. The only time the app touches the internet is a quiet weekly check for a new version, which you can ignore.

Why is the download ~1.1 GB?

It bundles everything it needs to work offline — the FFmpeg media tools and a full LibreOffice engine for faithful Word/Excel/PowerPoint conversions. Nothing to install separately, nothing to configure. That self-contained bundle is most of the size.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

It bundles FFmpeg, which is GPL-licensed and incompatible with the App Store's terms. A notarized direct download is the honest way to ship it — it's signed by Apple's notary service, so it opens cleanly with a normal double-click.

Will it run on my Mac?

It needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer). It won't open on older Intel Macs — the bundled media and office tools are Apple-Silicon-only.

How do I uninstall it?

Drag the app to the Trash. That's it — there's no background installer, login item, or system extension left behind. Everything lives inside the app itself.
