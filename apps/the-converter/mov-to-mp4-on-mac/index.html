How to convert MOV to MP4 on a Mac

iPhone videos and Mac screen recordings are saved as MOV. Windows PCs, Android phones and a lot of upload forms would rather have MP4. You can convert them on your Mac without uploading anything.

With what's already on your Mac

macOS doesn't have a one-step MOV to MP4 converter. QuickTime Player's File › Export As makes a new copy, but it's still a MOV.

iMovie, one video at a time

  1. Open iMovie (free from Apple) and start a new movie.
  2. Drag the MOV into the timeline.
  3. Choose File › Share › File, pick a resolution and quality, and save.

At its usual quality settings iMovie saves an MP4. It's a lot of steps for a single clip, and you repeat them for every video.

With The Converter

The Converter is a free, open-source Mac app that converts images, video, audio and documents entirely on your Mac.

  1. Drop the videos onto The Converter's window.
  2. Pick MP4.
  3. Click Convert. You'll see each video's progress as it goes.

Or skip the window: select the files in Finder, Control-click, and choose Services › Convert to MP4. The new file lands next to the original.

What you get

  • H.264 video, which plays on practically everything. iPhone videos recorded in HEVC come out as H.264 too.
  • On the default High quality setting the result looks the same as the original (H.264 at CRF 20). Balanced and Smaller files make smaller files.
  • AAC audio at 192 kbps.
  • The file is arranged so it starts playing in a browser before it has fully downloaded.
  • Select fifty videos in Finder and it works through them a few at a time.

The Converter is free and open source. It needs macOS 14 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

Questions

Is MOV worse than MP4?

No. They're both containers, and MP4 was built on QuickTime's MOV format. The difference is which apps and devices expect which one.

Can't I just rename .mov to .mp4?

Sometimes the renamed file plays, but nothing inside it has changed. If the video is HEVC, as most iPhone videos are, it still won't play where HEVC isn't supported. Converting re-encodes it as H.264.

Why does it take a while?

The Converter re-encodes the video rather than relabelling it, which is what makes the MP4 play everywhere. A long 4K video takes longer than a short screen recording.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your Mac, with no file-size limit.
