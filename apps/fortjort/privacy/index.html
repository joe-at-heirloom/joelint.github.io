Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Fortjort is made by The Peninsular Company, LLC. This page explains what the game does with your information, which is very little.

What we collect

Nothing. Fortjort has no accounts, shows no ads, and has no third-party tracking or analytics. We don't collect, store or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your kingdom's progress and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app (or, on the web, clearing the site's data) deletes them.

Purchases

The in-app purchase that unlocks the whole kingdom is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy covers that purchase.

The web version

The free web version is served by Cloudflare, which handles standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver it. We don't keep or use that data.

The support form

If you write to us from the support page, your email address and message are sent through Formspree so we can reply. To keep out spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person, not a bot, is sending it. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers that check. We use what you send only to answer you.

Children

Fortjort doesn't collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes (say, to count anonymous gameplay statistics), we'll update this page and the game's App Store privacy details first.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the support page.
