Support

Stuck, found a bug, or lost your purchase? Here's how to sort it, and how to reach us.

I bought the whole kingdom, but it's locked again

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase (or tap Restore on the unlock screen), signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. It works on every device signed in to that account.

How do I unlock the rest of the kingdom?

The Meadow and Millbrook are free. Pause the game (the button at the top left) and tap Kingdom, then Unlock the whole kingdom. It's a single $4.99 purchase for four more lands and their bosses.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your kingdom with it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Only buying or restoring the whole kingdom needs a connection.

Contact us

Tell us your device and what happened, and we'll get back to you by email.
