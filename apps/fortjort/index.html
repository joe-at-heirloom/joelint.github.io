What you do

Build

Barracks, archer towers, catapults and treasuries, behind walls that grow from logs to stone to iron.

Fight

Raids hit at dusk: brutes, shield walls, siege rams and shamans. Six bosses, from the Warlord to the Raider King.

Explore

Six lands, from the Meadow to Frost Pass and Raider Keep. March on with your squad, your gold and your upgrades.

Play your way

Easy, Normal and Hard, plus Chris mode, where one blow brings down the Keep. Plays offline.
